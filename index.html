About

I am currently a visiting fellow at DAMTP, University of Cambridge, working with Prof I. David Abrahams, and a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at University College London, supervised by Prof Helen J. Wilson. I commenced my PhD in 2022 on a four-year fully funded studentship, directly following my bachelor's degree, and am due to graduate later this year.

I have broad interests in analytical and numerical methods in applied mathematics, particularly their applications to fluid mechanics and complex physical systems, with a focus on modelling underlying physical mechanisms.

During my undergraduate studies, I began participating in interdisciplinary research in 2018 and pursuing mathematical research in 2021. I was ranked among the top 5% of students in my cohort, placed in the top 1% of participants worldwide in the Mathematical Contest in Modeling, received scholarships and awards each year (more than 30 in total), and published a research paper.

Throughout my PhD, I have conducted mathematical research resulting in one publication in the Journal of Fluid Mechanics and three further manuscripts in preparation based on completed research, presented my work at international conferences, and received awards and funding each year, including the David Crighton Fellowship (2026), Wren Fund (2025), QJMAM Fund (2024), and Lighthill Fund (2023).
